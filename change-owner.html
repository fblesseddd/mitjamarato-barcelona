Regulations Regarding Change of Ownership

  • Deadline: The change of bib owner can be made until January 15th, 2027 at 23:59h.
  • Transfer fee: Changes completed before December 15th are free of charge. Subsequent modifications carry a €5 administrative fee.
  • Cancellation of Previous Bib: The previous bib code is automatically invalidated once transferred to ensure race security.
  • Expo Pick-Up: The new owner must present an official photo ID (passport, national ID card) and the confirmation voucher at the Expo Sports to collect the bib number and runner's bag.
