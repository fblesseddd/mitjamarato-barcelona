What is your Beat?

In Barcelona, a city where diversity runs deep, every runner finds their own rhythm, their own beat, within this urban symphony.

What is your Beat? — it's the question that invites us to look inward and define how we make this journey ours. Because it's not just about running — it's about connecting. With the city. With others. With ourselves.

Some start their day running at sunrise, then reward themselves with a cozy breakfast. Others dive into the energy of the crowd. Because running the Mitja isn't just about moving forward… It's about elevating the experience. Pushing your limits. Leveling up.

Frequently asked questions

Solve your doubts and questions about the Hyundai Mitja Marató Barcelona by Brooks and stand at the starting line with all the guarantees.

How can I find out my bib number?

You'll receive an email on race week with your bib number (check your inbox as spam).

If you haven't received the email, you can access it through our official website, through the following link.

If none of these options have worked for you, you should go to Athlete service once you're at Expo Sports.

Where do I have to pick up my bib number?

You should pick up your bib number at Expo Sports, both on Thursday, Friday and Saturday before the race.

Can another person pick up my bib number?

Yes. Race bibs and timing chips will be distributed solely and exclusively in person at the Sports Expo on the Thursday, Friday, and Saturday leading up to the race.

Bibs will not be distributed on race day. Runners may authorize a third party to collect their bib and runner's bag. This person must present the confirmation slip showing the bib number and the runner's ID (digital copies are accepted).

Bibs will not be issued to anyone who fails to meet the requirements set by the organizers. After collecting the bib at the designated location, runners may pick up their T-shirt on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to size availability.

Article 10

Can I collect my race number on the day of the race?

No. The established schedules are as follows: Thursday 11/02 from 3 PM to 8 PM, Friday 12/02 from 2 PM to 8 PM, and Saturday 13/02 from 10 AM to 8 PM.
