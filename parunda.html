PaRunda by Brooks!

The party before the race! Shake out your legs, enjoy good music, and warm up with fellow runners before race day.

🔥 JOIN THE PARUNDA EXPERIENCE

Run, Groove & Warm Up in Barcelona

The PaRunda by Brooks is Barcelona’s official pre-race 5K shakeout run and celebration. Held on Saturday morning before Sunday's half marathon, it gathers runners from over 100 nationalities for a relaxed jog, lively DJ sets, breakfast, and exclusive Brooks gear testing.

Whether you're aiming for a personal best on Sunday or just visiting Barcelona for the half marathon weekend, PaRunda gives you the ultimate community vibe.

Event Details

📍 Start & Finish: Parc de la Ciutadella, Barcelona

📅 Date: Saturday, February 13, 2027

⏰ Time: 10:00 AM

🏃 Distance: 5 km (Non-competitive shakeout pace)

🎁 Included: Official Brooks technical shirt, runner refreshment breakfast, and live DJ set.

👟

Brooks Shoe Trials

Test the latest Brooks running footwear models like the Hyperion Max and Ghost Max right on the course.

🎵

Live Music & Vibes

DJs, percussionists, and entertainment will turn the Ciutadella park into a huge running fiesta.

🥐

Post-Run Breakfast

Fresh fruit, croissants, recovery drinks, and coffee courtesy of official race provisioning partners.
