Race Rules & Official Regulations

Read the official sporting and organizational terms governing the Hyundai Mitja Marató Barcelona by Brooks 2027.

Article 1: Organization & Governing Bodies

The Hyundai Mitja Marató Barcelona by Brooks is organized by RPM Sports and the Barcelona City Council (Institut Barcelona Esports). The event conforms to World Athletics and RFEA (Royal Spanish Athletics Federation) road racing regulations.

Article 2: Date, Route & Time Limit

The race will be held on Sunday, February 14th, 2027. The distance is 21.097 km officially certified by IAAF/AIMS. The maximum official time limit to complete the course is 2 hours and 45 minutes from the start of the final wave.

Article 3: Participation & Age Restrictions

The race is open to all runners of legal age (at least 18 years old on race day). Minors are strictly prohibited from participating. Each participant competes under their own physical responsibility and confirms their adequate medical preparedness.

Article 4: Bib Numbers, Chips & Change of Owner

The bib number is personal and non-transferable without using the official transfer platform. If a participant cannot attend, they may use the official Change Owner system up until January 15th, 2027. Running with another person's bib without official transfer leads to automatic disqualification and suspension.

Article 5: Refreshment & Medical Stations

Refreshment stations will be situated along the route at approximately km 5, 10, 15, and at the finish line, providing water, isotonic sports drinks, and fruit. Official medical services and ambulances will monitor the entire route.
